Pocket Pager is the restaurant pager on your guests' own phones: they scan a code, get an order number, and their phone rings the moment the order is ready. No pagers to buy or lose, no crowd at the counter.

How it works
• Guests scan the code at the counter with the camera, no app download needed.
• Their order number shows at once, with how many orders are ahead and the wait time.
• The cashier taps "Ready", and the guest's phone rings and vibrates, even when locked.

For restaurant and café owners
• Sign your restaurant up in two minutes, in 3 simple steps, with a hint next to every field.
• Get the cashier PIN and the links to the cashier and QR screens right away.
• Follow every branch: today's orders, late orders, prep time and busy hours.
• Add a manager per branch who sees that branch only, and cashiers with their own PIN.
• Send a message to every branch's cashier screen and see who read it.
• Pause a branch with one tap.

Features that sell more
• Delivery with your own drivers: guests follow the order to their door.
• Stamp card: a stamp per order and a gift after a few, no paper cards.
• A promo while guests wait, under their order number.
• Google review after pickup.
• POS link and a QR code on the receipt.

Made for cafés, restaurants, fast food, bakeries and any place where guests wait for their order.
