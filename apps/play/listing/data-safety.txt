Google Play > App content > Data safety

Does your app collect or share user data? Yes, collects. Shares: No.
Is all data encrypted in transit? Yes (HTTPS only).
Can users request deletion? Yes: in the app, «حسابي» -> «اقفل الحساب».

Collected:
- Personal info > Name: collected, required, App functionality, Account management. Not shared.
- Personal info > Phone number: collected, required, App functionality, Account management. Not shared.
- App activity > Other actions (orders, settings of the restaurant): App functionality. Not shared.
Not collected: location, contacts, photos (the logo is uploaded by choice, stored for the customer page), financial info, device IDs, advertising ID.
